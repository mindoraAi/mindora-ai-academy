What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the ability of computer systems to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Examples of AI capabilities

  • Learning from data.
  • Recognising patterns.
  • Understanding human language.
  • Solving problems.

How does AI work?

AI systems process information and use algorithms and models to produce outputs based on data and instructions.

AI and traditional programming

Traditional programming generally follows explicitly written instructions. Some AI approaches learn patterns from data during training.
